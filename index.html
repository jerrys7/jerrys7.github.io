About

I’m a Ph.D. student in Natural Resources and Environmental Sciences at the University of Illinois Urbana-Champaign, where I work at the intersection of artificial intelligence and agriculture.

I earned my B.S. in Computer Science and Environmental Science from UC Berkeley in 2024. I then spent a year as a Software Engineer at Google Cloud, building features for the launch of Google’s Trusted Partner Cloud in France, before returning to academia.

Research

2026–present

Agroecosystem Sustainability Center

Process-based modeling of rice agroecosystems

I use the ecosys process-based ecosystem model to simulate rice agroecosystems. Advised by Professor Kaiyu Guan.

2024–2026

Pau Lab · UC Berkeley

Hyperspectral diversity and plant communities

I analyze how hyperspectral diversity metrics—including coefficients of variation and convex-hull volumes—relate to plant lineage, trait, and species diversity. The work uses spectral preprocessing such as Savitzky–Golay filtering and continuum removal. Advised by Professor Stephanie Pau.

2024–2026

Gherardi Lab · UC Berkeley

Satellite estimates of rangeland productivity

I compare satellite-derived net primary productivity with in-situ above- and below-ground measurements, using Rangeland Analysis Platform and Planet data to study where remote estimates succeed and where they can improve. Advised by Professor Laureano A. Gherardi.

2022–2023

Wang Lab · UC Berkeley

Thermal ecology of Aegean wall lizards

I studied orange, yellow, and white color morphs across the Greek Cyclades to understand how thermal physiology and microhabitat variability may help maintain phenotypic diversity under climate change. Advised by Professor Kinsey Brock.
